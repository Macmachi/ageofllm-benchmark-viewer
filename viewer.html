Goal of the game

A 1v1 duel between two AIs. Each turn both players take up to 3 actions. Win by destroying the enemy base before the turn limit (default 80) — by nuclear bomb or military conquest (tanks reduce the base to 0 HP) — or by an accepted ultimatum. A peace or the turn limit ends in a draw. Fog of war hides the enemy half: you must scout.

Who plays first is drawn at random on turn 1, then alternates every turn. Each side starts with 5◆ and 0☢ and earns +1◆/turn passively, on top of its mines.

Triangle: Fighter > Tank/Drone/Fighter · Tank > SAM/Tank/buildings · SAM > air units. Only the Tank can hit buildings (2 HP/hit). Attacker survives (mirror rule). Line of sight: a ground attack (tank/sam) is blocked by a mountain or building between shooter and target — flank around cover. The fighter (air) ignores obstacles. Ground movement needs a clear path: mountains and buildings block it, so being in range is not enough.

Deployment: units roll off the base onto a free adjacent cell the engine picks (production fails if none is free), and a unit produced this turn cannot move or attack until its owner's next turn — the same one-turn delay as a building.

Construction: operational on the next turn. Under construction = no effect + destroyed in 1 hit. You can only build on a visible, free cell. One building per cell; cells adjacent to a base are reserved for unit spawning. Mines can go on any free matching deposit you see — incl. the enemy's side (no instant capture: destroy the enemy mine or wait for its deposit to run dry, then claim it); the silo stays own-territory only. Deposits deplete: a drained deposit removes its mine and respawns elsewhere on the same side (central on column 6).

Nuclear bomb

Cost 25☢, dropping 2☢ on turn 40 and every 10 turns after: 23☢ from T40, 21☢ from 50, 19☢ from 60, 17☢ from 70, 15☢ from 80. The engine floor of 13☢ is only reached on turn 90, so a standard 80-turn match never goes below 15☢. Requires an operational silo + uranium ≥ cost + enemy base discovered. Launches are secret & simultaneous: resolved at end of turn — a double launch = mutual destruction.

Early warning: a bomb cannot be intercepted; the only answer is to fire back the same turn. A player is told of an enemy launch only if an enemy silo is in its field of view at that moment — so only the player acting second that turn can ever be warned. Without that line of sight the launch stays secret.

Proposals are binding only if accepted; a promise in a message is not. A ceasefire accepted on turn T is active on T, T+1, T+2 and T+3 — attacks are forbidden for both sides and resume on T+4. It cannot be broken early by a conventional attack; the one thing it does not stop is the bomb, which may still be launched (with the +6☢ penalty). Peace and ultimatum stay open during it. An ultimatum's deadline must fall within the next 3 turns, and refusing one costs nothing.

Map

The central barrier (column 6), the in-territory mountains and all deposit positions are seed-driven — they differ every match. Mountains block ground movement, construction and line of sight for ground attacks (air flies over). Extra mountains are confined to cols 2-4 / 8-10; the cell immediately in front of each col.6 passage (col.5 side A, col.7 side B) is always kept clear so neither side's access to the central deposit is randomly sealed.
